Welcome — this is my personal trail log.

I ride a side-by-side (SxS) through the backcountry of Nova Scotia's South Shore, mostly around Lunenburg County. Every entry here is a real ride: the GPS track straight off my Garmin watch, the distance and elevation stats, and photos from along the way.

Have a poke around the [trails](/trails) and [lists](/lists) to see where the machines have been.
